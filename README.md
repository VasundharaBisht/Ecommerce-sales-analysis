# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

This project analyzes an e-commerce dataset containing **31,047 orders** with total revenue of **₹2.11 Cr**.  
The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, customer demographics, order status, category trends, and channel contribution.

---

## 🚀 Project Objectives
- Understand key business metrics (Total Sales, Orders, AOV)
- Analyze customer segmentation (gender, age group)
- Evaluate monthly sales trends
- Identify top-performing states
- Review channel performance across platforms
- Assess operational metrics (delivery, return, cancellation)

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Excel** (Dashboard & Data Visualization)  
- **Excel** (Data Cleaning & Preparation)  
- **GitHub** (Project Hosting)

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Total revenue: **₹2.11 Cr** from **31K+ orders**
- Women contribute **64%** of total sales  
- Adults (25–45) form the biggest customer segment  
- **92% delivery success rate** (strong operations)
- Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra contribute **80%+** of orders  
- Top states: **Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh**
